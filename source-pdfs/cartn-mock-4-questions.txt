 ​ e​​are​​evaluating​​a​​shift​​to​​a​​circular​​business​​model​​for​​the​​Bio-Shield​​range.​​Instead​​of​

​Proposed Mechanics:​

    ​●​ ​Partnership:​ ​Partner​ ​with​ ​major​ ​supermarket​ ​retailers​ ​to​ ​install​ ​Cartn-Collection​
           ​Points.​

​Reference material​
​Project Committee Email​
​To:​​Mario Baquero, Finance Director​
 ​From:​​Project Lead, Bio-Shield Circular Initiative​
  ​Date:​​1 April 2026​
   ​Subject:​​URGENT: Bio-Shield Infrastructure Failure & Regulatory Risk​

​Mario,​

​We​​have​​completed​​the​​3-month​​"Take-Back"​​pilot​​in​​Central​​City,​​and​​the​​results​​indicate​
​that the current project trajectory is unsustainable due to the risks highlighted below.​

​1. Pilot Performance & Viability Gap​

​The​ ​consumer​ ​return​ ​rate​ ​during​ ​the​ ​pilot​ ​was​ ​only​ ​22%,​ ​significantly​ ​below​ ​the​ ​40%​
 ​threshold​ ​required​ ​for​ ​the​ ​logistics​ ​to​ ​be​ ​cost-neutral.​ ​Furthermore,​ ​a​​confidential​​legal​
  ​briefing​ ​suggests​ ​that​ ​the​ ​upcoming​ ​"Harrland​ ​Green-Tax"​​will​​only​​exempt​​starch-based​
   ​products​ ​if​ ​we​ ​can​​prove​​a​​50%​​recovery​​rate.​​If​​we​​fail​​to​​hit​​this​​50%​​target,​​we​​face​​a​
​H$500 per tonne tax, effectively neutralising our competitive price advantage.​

​2. IT & Logistics Deadlock​

​The​​IT​​tracking​​system​​is​​now​​3​​months​​behind​​schedule.​​Friction​​between​​IT​​(focusing​​on​
 ​high-security​​encryption)​​and​​Logistics​​(claiming​​the​​interface​​is​​too​​complex​​for​​drivers)​
  ​has​​stalled​​development.​​We​​have​​no​​formal​​mechanism​​to​​resolve​​these​​cross-functional​
​disputes.​

​3. Scope Creep & Budget Overrun​

​The​​project​​is​​suffering​​from​​significant​​scope​​creep​​due​​to​​unauthorised​​feature​​requests​
 ​from​ ​the​ ​marketing​ ​department​ ​-​ ​most​ ​notably​ ​a​ ​late-stage​ ​requirement​ ​for​ ​individual​
  ​customer​​carbon-tracking.​​This​​has​​caused​​the​​IT​​budget​​to​​balloon​​from​​H$1.2​​million​​to​
   ​H$2.8​ ​million.​ ​Currently,​ ​these​ ​changes​ ​are​ ​being​ ​requested​ ​via​ ​email​ ​directly​ ​to​
​developers, bypassing both the Project Manager and Finance.​
