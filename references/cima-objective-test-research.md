# CIMA objective-test research

Reviewed 26 August 2026 for AFT practice-set design.

## Findings

The official AICPA & CIMA exam-blueprints page states that the CGMA Examination Blueprints are based on the syllabus and describe what is examinable in nine Objective Tests and three Case Study Examinations. It also states that the blueprints are published annually and provide assessment format, structure, and weightings. The page groups the Objective Tests by professional level: Operational E1/P1/F1, Management E2/P2/F2, and Strategic E3/P3/F3.

The official CGMA Exams resources page describes itself as the place for exam dates, fees, deadlines, and booking information. Search results for the official CGMA exam resources also identify Objective Tests as using a range of item formats, including short multiple-choice questions, number-entry questions, drag-and-drop questions, and other formats. AFT practice content will therefore be labelled as AFT-created and illustrative, not official CIMA material, and will focus on single-best-answer practice for this implementation slice.

## Product-design implication

Create three purchasable AFT objective-test products aligned by level and clearly marked as AFT-created practice: Operational Objective Practice Set, Management Objective Practice Set, and Strategic Objective Practice Set. Do not use official CIMA questions, logos, or claims of endorsement. Each set should contain a manageable, configurable bank of original practice questions and inherit the admin-controlled access period, defaulting to 30 days.
